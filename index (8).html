Questions, answered

✦

Use the booking form above or message us on WhatsApp at 0729 595 601 with the service and the day you prefer. We will confirm a time.

We are in Kitale at Ebby Towers, 2nd Floor, Room D4. Use the Find us card in the About section for directions.

Appointments get priority and a guaranteed time. Walk-ins are welcome when there is space, so message us first to save a wait.

We accept M-Pesa and cash.

Microshading typically lasts one to three years depending on your skin type and aftercare. A touch-up keeps the colour fresh. Ask us about your skin when you book.

For brows, keep the area dry and clean for the first days and avoid picking. For lashes, avoid oil-based products and brush them gently. For nails, wear gloves for cleaning. We will give you specific tips after your visit.
